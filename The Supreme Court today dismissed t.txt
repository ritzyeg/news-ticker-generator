The Supreme Court today dismissed the petition seeking an independent probe into the electoral bonds data. A five-judge constitutional bench headed by the Chief Justice observed that the Election Commission has already published all relevant transaction disclosures.

---

Indian Railways announced the introduction of twenty new Vande Bharat Express sleeper trains across high-density metro corridors by early next year. Railway officials confirmed that trial runs for the indigenous trainsets will commence next month.

---

Gold prices surged to a new record high of sixty-five thousand rupees per sovereign in domestic markets today following volatile movements in international trading. Jewelers attributed the sudden rally to global geopolitical tensions and safe-haven buying.

---

The Indian cricket team secured a thrilling five-wicket victory against Australia in the final test match at Ahmedabad to clinch the bilateral series. Rohit Sharma was declared player of the match for his decisive second-innings century.
